# CHEA Website Narrative & Program Strategy (Carrd-ready)

## 1) Positioning
CHEA Institute should present as a data-informed NGO focused on high-need, high-trust sectors in Cambodia:
- Child nutrition
- Foundational literacy
- School WASH

These sectors are repeatedly highlighted by UNICEF and World Bank as major constraints on child wellbeing and long-term opportunity in Cambodia.

## 2) Evidence Snapshot for Storytelling
- UNICEF (June 13, 2025): child stunting improved from 32% to 22% (2014–2021), but wasting remains around 1 in 10 children under five.
- UNICEF Cambodia: WASH and hygiene deficits are tied to child illness, stunting, and school/health risks.
- World Bank (2026 results brief): only a small share of Grade 5 students read proficiently; foundational learning is a key bottleneck.

## 3) Main-domain Story Arc (cheainstitute.org)
1. Problem: preventable nutrition + learning loss
2. Proof: nationally documented indicators + partner-aligned approach
3. Action: 3 flagship programs with clear targets
4. Trust: transparent impact dashboards and quarterly reports
5. Participation: donate, partner, volunteer, sponsor a school

## 4) Sub-domain Story Arc (youth.cheainstitute.org)
- Identity: youth-powered initiative under CHEA umbrella
- Founder message: Munica as youth convener (not personal admissions narrative)
- Programs: school chapter model + summer campaign + impact lab
- Continuity: high school to college chapter bridge
- Accountability: campaign-level dashboards linked to main CHEA reporting

- "Healthy Start Classrooms" (nutrition + hygiene kits)
- "Read to Rise Cambodia" (books + reading clubs)
- "Safe Water, Strong Futures" (school handwashing and sanitation)

Rationale: child-focused, tangible deliverables and measurable outcomes typically convert better than abstract institutional asks.
