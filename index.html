CHEA Institute • Cambodia Impact Hub

From Compassion to Measurable Impact in Cambodia

CHEA Institute is a U.S.-based nonprofit initiative partnering with local leaders in Cambodia to improve childhood nutrition, foundational learning, and safe school environments. We design programs that are simple to fund, transparent to track, and built for long-term local ownership.

Why this focus now

Child nutrition is urgent

Cambodia reduced child stunting from 32% to 22% (2014–2021), yet wasting remains around 1 in 10 children under five—one of the most life-threatening forms of malnutrition.

Learning gaps are severe

World Bank reporting highlights that only a small share of Grade 5 students can read proficiently, limiting future opportunity and national competitiveness.

Integrated action works

UNICEF and government programs show better results when nutrition, WASH, and education are addressed together at community level.

Our Three Flagship Programs

1) Healthy Start Classrooms

School-linked nutrition screening, referral, and caregiver learning kits for children under 10. Target: 2 provinces, 20 schools, 2,000 children.

2) Read to Rise Cambodia

After-school foundational literacy clubs with local mentors and bilingual take-home reading packs. Target: 1,500 students annually.

3) Safe Water, Strong Futures

Low-cost handwashing stations, hygiene curriculum, and school WASH maintenance micro-grants. Target: 30 schools in 18 months.
